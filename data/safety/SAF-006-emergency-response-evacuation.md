# SAF-006 — Emergency Response and Evacuation

## 1. Emergency contacts

| Situation | Contact | Extension |
|---|---|---|
| Medical — injury requiring more than a bandage | Plant nurse | 4110 |
| Fire, large spill, gas odor, release to drain | Plant emergency line | 4111 |
| Life-threatening emergency | External emergency services | 911, then 4111 |
| Security / after-hours | Guard house | 4100 |

The plant nurse is on site 06:00–22:00, Monday through Friday. Outside those hours, the shift
supervisor is the designated first responder and the emergency line routes to their radio.

## 2. Alarm signals

| Signal | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Continuous horn + white strobe | Fire / evacuate | Evacuate to assembly point |
| Three short blasts, repeating | Shelter in place (severe weather) | Move to interior shelter areas |
| Intermittent horn + amber strobe | Chemical release, area isolation | Await PA instruction; do not evacuate through the affected bay |
| Voice PA announcement | Overrides all of the above | Follow the announcement |

## 3. Evacuation

1. Press **Cycle Stop** if you can do so in under 5 seconds. Do not attempt to complete a
   cycle, finish a part, or shut down heats. **Equipment is replaceable.**
2. Leave belongings. Do not use the freight elevator.
3. Exit by the nearest marked route. Primary and secondary routes are posted at every cell.
4. Proceed to your assembly point and remain there until released by a warden.

### Assembly points

| Area | Assembly point |
|---|---|
| Bays 1–4 (molding) | **Point A** — north parking lot, by the flagpole |
| Bays 5–8 (molding, granulation) | **Point B** — east lot, beyond the truck court |
| Tool room, maintenance shop | **Point B** |
| Warehouse and shipping | **Point C** — west lot |
| Office and QC lab | **Point A** |

Do not cross the truck court to reach a different point. Report to whichever point your
exit leads to and tell the warden where you normally work.

## 4. Headcount

Each area has a designated evacuation warden (identified by an orange vest in the cell's
supply cabinet). Wardens sweep their area, then take headcount at the assembly point using
the shift roster printed at the start of each shift.

**Supervisors are responsible for accounting for visitors and contractors in their area.**
Report any unaccounted person to the warden immediately — never re-enter the building to
search. Re-entry is authorized only by the incident commander.

## 5. Fire

- **Only** attempt a fire if it is smaller than a waste basket, you have a clear exit behind
  you, you are trained, and you have raised the alarm first.
- Extinguisher classes: ABC dry chemical throughout the floor; **CO2 units at the electrical
  cabinets and hot runner controllers** — do not use water or dry chemical on energized
  electrical equipment.
- Hydraulic oil fire: Class B. Use ABC or CO2, never water.
- After **any** extinguisher discharge, however small, the unit is tagged and sent for
  recharge and an incident report is filed.

## 6. Medical emergency

1. Do not move an injured person unless they are in immediate further danger.
2. Send someone to call ext. 4110 (or 911 for life-threatening) while you stay with them.
3. Send a second person to the nearest entrance to meet and guide responders.
4. Apply first aid within the limits of your training. AEDs are mounted at the north aisle,
   the tool crib, and outside the QC lab.
5. Preserve the scene. Do not restart or clean the equipment until EHS releases it.

For thermal burns specifically, follow the first-aid sequence in **SAF-003 §7** — the
handling of adhered molten polymer differs from an ordinary burn.

## 7. Severe weather

On the shelter-in-place signal, move to the interior restrooms, the tool crib, or the
locker room corridor. Stay away from the skylights in Bays 6–8 and from all exterior
overhead doors. Remain until the all-clear is given by voice PA — not until the horn stops.

## 8. Drills and readiness

- Full evacuation drill: **twice per year**, one per shift rotation.
- Shelter-in-place drill: **annually**.
- Warden refresher training: **annually**.
- Exit routes, extinguishers, and AED cabinets are inspected **monthly** and must be kept
  clear at all times. Staging a gaylord, pallet, or scrap bin in an exit route is an
  immediately correctable violation — move it and report it.

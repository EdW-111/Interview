# SAF-005 — PPE Requirements Matrix

## 1. Baseline PPE — entire production floor

Required of **everyone** past the yellow line at the floor entrance, including visitors,
auditors, and office staff:

- ANSI-rated safety glasses with side shields (prescription glasses alone do not qualify)
- Closed-toe, slip-resistant safety footwear with a protective toe cap
- Hearing protection in posted high-noise zones (granulator area GR-40, Bays 6–8)
- No loose clothing, no dangling jewelry, no lanyards worn around the neck near rotating equipment
- Long hair tied back and contained

## 3. Glove selection guidance

Glove choice is a common source of confusion. Use this decision order:

1. **Is the hazard heat above 50 °C?** → High-temperature gauntlet gloves. Cut-resistant
   gloves offer no thermal protection and will conduct heat.
2. **Is the hazard a chemical?** → Nitrile, chemical-resistant. Check the SDS for breakthrough
   time; replace immediately on any splash.
3. **Is the hazard a sharp edge (flash, sprue, mold surface)?** → Cut-resistant, level A4.
4. **Is the concern part cleanliness?** → Lint-free cotton or clean nitrile.

**Never wear any glove near rotating equipment** — granulator infeed, unguarded conveyor
rollers, or a lathe in the tool room. The entrapment risk exceeds the contact risk.

## 4. Inspection, replacement, and cost

- Inspect gloves and face shields before each use. Discard any glove with a tear, stiffening,
  or heat glazing; discard face shields that are crazed, scratched, or impact-marked.
- Replacement PPE is stocked in the crib and issued at **no cost to the employee**. There is
  no approval needed and no limit on safety-critical replacements. An employee is never
  expected to work with degraded PPE because a replacement was inconvenient to obtain.
- Prescription safety glasses are provided through the plant's vision program; see HR.

## 5. Visitors and contractors

Visitors are issued glasses and a visitor vest at reception and must be escorted at all times.
Contractors provide their own PPE meeting or exceeding this matrix and must present it at
the safety orientation before their first entry.

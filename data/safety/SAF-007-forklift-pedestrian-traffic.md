# SAF-007 — Powered Industrial Trucks and Pedestrian Traffic

## 1. Authorization

Only employees holding a current Northgate powered industrial truck (PIT) certification may
operate a forklift, reach truck, or powered pallet jack. Certification is valid for
**3 years** and requires re-evaluation after any incident, near-miss, or observed unsafe
operation. Certification is equipment-class specific — a sit-down counterbalance card does
not authorize a reach truck.

## 2. Pre-use inspection

A documented inspection is required **at the start of every shift**, before first use, on
Form SAF-007-F1. Checks include:

- Forks: no cracks, no bent tips, positive locking pins seated
- Tires, mast chains, and hydraulic hoses: no damage, no leaks
- Horn, lights, backup alarm, seatbelt
- Brakes and parking brake
- Battery/LPG condition, no damage to the connector
- Data plate legible and capacity rating readable

**Any defect affecting safe operation takes the truck out of service immediately.** Tag it,
park it in the maintenance bay, and notify the supervisor. Do not "finish this one load."

## 3. Operating rules

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Speed limit, production floor | 5 mph (8 km/h) |
| Speed limit, pedestrian-marked zones and near cells | 3 mph (5 km/h) |
| Minimum clearance from a pedestrian | 3 m (10 ft) |
| Travel with load | Forks 100–150 mm off the floor, mast tilted back |
| Grade travel | Load uphill; travel in reverse when descending with a load |

- Sound the horn at every blind corner, every aisle intersection, and before backing.
- Travel in reverse when the load blocks forward visibility.
- Never lift a person on the forks. Never allow a rider.
- Never travel with a raised load.
- Park with forks lowered flat, mast forward, controls neutral, parking brake set, key
  removed if leaving the operator's view.

## 4. Pedestrian rules

- Walk in the marked green pedestrian lanes wherever they exist.
- **Make eye contact with the operator before crossing in front of any truck.** If you have
  not made eye contact, assume the operator has not seen you.
- Do not use a phone, tablet, or radio display while walking on the floor.
- Never walk under a raised load or an overhead crane load.
- Pedestrians have right of way, but a forklift's stopping distance means the pedestrian
  must still yield when in doubt.

## 5. Blind corners and high-traffic points

Convex mirrors are installed at the Bay 4 / north aisle intersection, the tool room door,
and the warehouse-to-floor transition. Mirrors are inspected monthly; a broken or fogged
mirror is a **P2** work order.

The dock-to-Bay-5 crossing is the highest-frequency pedestrian/forklift conflict at Plant 4.
Both parties come to a **full stop** at the painted stop line.

## 6. Load handling

- Never exceed the truck's rated capacity for the load center shown on the data plate.
  Capacity **decreases** as load center distance and lift height increase.
- Gaylords of resin are stacked no more than **2 high** in staging, **3 high** in racking.
- Damaged, wet, or bulging gaylords are not stacked at all — move to the quarantine area
  and notify QC (see QC-005).
- Do not push or drag a load with the forks.

## 7. Overhead crane and mold handling

Mold handling with the bay crane is the highest-consequence lifting task at Plant 4.

- Only trained riggers may rig a lift. One designated signal person per lift; the crane
  operator takes signals from that person only — except a **stop signal, which is accepted
  from anyone**.
- Bay crane rated capacity is **3,200 kg**. Verify the mold's stamped weight before rigging;
  if the stamp is missing or illegible, do not lift — consult the tool record.
- Inspect slings, shackles, and eyebolts before every lift. Remove from service any sling
  with cuts, broken strands, heat damage, or a missing/illegible capacity tag.
- Lift eyebolts must be fully seated and rated for the mold half's weight.
- **Never stand or reach under a suspended load.** Establish an exclusion zone and keep hands
  off the load — use a tag line to control swing.
- Lift 50 mm, stop, and confirm balance and brake hold before continuing.

Detailed mold change sequencing is in **MNT-002**; this section governs the lifting portion only.

## 8. Incident reporting

All PIT incidents are reportable, including racking strikes, dropped loads, and near-misses
with no damage. Near-miss reporting is explicitly **non-punitive** — the reporting employee
is not disciplined for reporting. Reports go to EHS on Form EHS-INC-01 within 24 hours.

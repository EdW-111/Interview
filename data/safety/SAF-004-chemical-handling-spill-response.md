# SAF-004 — Chemical Handling, Storage, and Spill Response

## 1. Hazard communication basics

Every container in Plant 4 must carry either the original manufacturer label or a completed
secondary-container label showing product name, hazard pictograms, and the responsible
department. **Unlabeled containers are treated as unknown hazardous waste** and are removed
by EHS at the department's cost.

Safety Data Sheets are available at every SDS station (north aisle, tool crib, maintenance
shop) and on the plant intranet. Any employee may request an SDS at any time and must be
given it without delay.

## 3. Storage rules

- Flammables are stored **only** in the yellow flammable cabinets. Maximum **20 L** of
  flammable liquid outside a cabinet on the production floor at any time.
- Never store any chemical on top of, or adjacent to, a press barrel, hot runner controller,
  or dryer hopper.
- Aerosols must not be stored above 50 °C or near the dryer exhaust.
- Incompatible pairs are segregated: **do not store oxidizers with solvents or oils.**
- Secondary containment is required for any container over 20 L.

## 4. Solvent use and ventilation

Mold cleaning with SC-3 is performed in the tool room at the ventilated bench, **not** at
the press, whenever the tool can be moved. When in-press cleaning is unavoidable:

- Use the portable extraction arm positioned within 300 mm of the work.
- Limit continuous use to 15 minutes.
- Ensure the barrel and hot runner are **below 40 °C** — solvent vapor near a hot surface is
  an ignition risk (see SAF-003 §2).
- No solvent use within 10 m of any hot work or grinding.

## 5. Spill response — the SWIM sequence

**S**top the source · **W**arn others · **I**solate the area · **M**anage the cleanup

### Small spill (< 20 L, known material, no injury)
Trained floor personnel may clean up using the nearest spill kit (yellow bins at Bays 2, 5,
and 8). Absorb with pads, place used pads in the labeled waste drum, and complete the spill
log at the EHS station.

### Large spill, unknown material, or any spill reaching a floor drain
**Do not attempt cleanup.** Evacuate the immediate area, call the emergency line at
extension **4111**, and follow SAF-006. Floor drains in Bays 1–4 discharge to the storm
system; any release to a drain is a reportable environmental event and must be reported to
EHS within **1 hour**.

### Hydraulic oil spills specifically
Hydraulic oil spills under a press are the most common spill at Plant 4 and are almost always
a symptom, not just a mess. After cleanup, raise a work order and reference MNT-003 §4 —
a recurring leak at the same fitting indicates a failing seal or a hose approaching end of life.
Use oil-only absorbent socks; do not use floor-dry granules within the guard envelope, as
they migrate into the tie-bar area and contaminate parts.

## 6. Waste

| Waste stream | Container | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Used oil | Black drum, "Used Oil" label | Bay 3 |
| Oil-contaminated absorbents/rags | Red drum, closed lid | Bay 3 |
| Solvent-contaminated rags | Yellow safety can, self-closing | Tool room |
| Purge (cooled, solid polymer) | Purge bin, then regrind or landfill per material | Bay 6 |
| Empty aerosols | Puncture bin | Aerosol cage |

Waste drums must be **closed except when actively adding waste** and must show an
accumulation start date. Drums are picked up when 80% full or at 90 days, whichever is first.
